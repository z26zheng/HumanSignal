# Product Copy & Positioning

## Decision: "Feels Human" Framing

Date: 2026-05-08

## Core Copy

**Product name:** HumanSignal

**Tagline:** Make LinkedIn feel human again.

**Chrome Web Store description:**
> HumanSignal adds private, on-device stickers to LinkedIn posts and comments so you can see what feels genuinely human and skip AI-generated noise.

**Install CTA:** Make LinkedIn feel human again.

## UI Copy Patterns

**Sticker tooltip:** "Signal: [label]"

**Popover header:** "[label]" with colored indicator dot

**Loading state:** "Scoring..."

**Unavailable state:** "Unavailable"

**Minimized state:** tiny colored dot (no text)

## Tone Guidelines

- Describe content, never judge authors.
- Use probabilistic language: "feels," "possibly," "likely."
- Never say "this person used AI," "this is fake," or "this is a bot."
- Explanations focus on what was observed, not who is at fault.
- Error states offer actionable guidance, not technical jargon.
